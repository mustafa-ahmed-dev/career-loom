# Localization

Two jobs: apply the target country's conventions, and edit any non-English text.

## Conventions
1. Read `../career-references/regions/README.md` and open only the folder for the target country. `pack.md` is the short summary; `tips.md` holds detail. Lite regions are labelled lite.
2. Every convention carries a status: **verified**, **secondary** or **unverified (U)**. Repeat that status when you apply it, and offer the user the choice when a rule is unverified.
3. Personal-data defaults: date of birth, nationality and marital status hidden by default; included on request where the region file says the norm includes them. Never national ID, passport or Iqama numbers. Photo is optional and hidden by default (`../career-references/tips/photo-and-personal-data.md`).
4. Gulf briefs include a dated localisation-quota check: not legal advice; verify with the ministry.
5. Format packs (US, UK, Europass, German Lebenslauf, French, Gulf, Iraq/KRI, India/South Asia, China, Korea, LatAm, academic, Japan rirekisho, UN) are separate from design systems.

## Language editing (required for any non-English output)
- Remove AI-sounding phrasing and stock phrases; keep the user's meaning and facts exactly. Do not add claims while translating.
- Default Arabic register: formal Modern Standard Arabic. Arabic and Sorani output needs native review; say so.
- Bilingual output is two separate files by default, or one mirrored document if sent directly to people.
- Use left-to-right isolates for emails, URLs and Latin terms inside right-to-left text; check punctuation, numerals and bullet direction.
- Kurdish: state the script and variant (Sorani Arabic script or Badini Latin) before writing.

## Read when needed
`../career-references/tips/multilingual-typography.md`.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only facts in the Fact Bank (`../career-references/templates/fact-bank.md`): user-stated, from an uploaded document, or user-estimated and shown with "~". Never invent or inflate a title, number, date or skill.
- Skills: Evidenced may go on the document; Latent needs one targeted question first; Gap never goes on it (offer a "currently learning" line).
- Never include salary history, ID or passport numbers, a full street address, skill percentages, employer logos, protected characteristics, reasons for leaving or "References available upon request".
- Minimize personal data. Nothing persists unless the user asks. Never auto-send or auto-apply.
- Be honest about status: regional conventions are verified / secondary / unverified; legal or quota points say "not legal advice; verify with the ministry"; scores are practice or risk indicators, never predictions.
- Full text: `../career-references/rules/integrity.md`. End every deliverable with the footer in `../career-references/SKILL.md`.
