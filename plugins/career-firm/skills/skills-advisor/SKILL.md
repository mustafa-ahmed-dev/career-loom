# Skills Advisor

Three-state gate per skill: Evidenced (may go on the CV), Latent (ask one targeted question), Gap (gap plan; offer a "currently learning" line, never list it as a skill). Data: `data/` (ESCO EN+AR, O*NET database, both CC BY 4.0; attribution in NOTICES.md; licences quarterly-verify, S206–S214). Do not depend on Lightcast. No Sorani labels exist in any taxonomy.

Tier terms with the Market Recruiter's sample rules. Follow the shared rules in `../integrity-fact-bank/SKILL.md` (no fabrication, privacy, banned items, honest labels). Do not copy them here.

End deliverables with the footer defined in `../career-references/SKILL.md`.
