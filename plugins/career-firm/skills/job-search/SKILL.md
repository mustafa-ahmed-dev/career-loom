# Job Search

Covers networking and outreach plus the weekly plan. The reality note in `career-firm` is the honest framing: silence is normal, referrals convert better, and success is measured in conversations per week, not applications sent.

## Ask
Who the user already knows (current and former colleagues, classmates, family contacts, community), target companies, how local employers usually hire in the target country, and hours available per week.

## Steps
1. **Channel plan.** Rank channels by who the user knows: referral, direct message to a hiring manager, recruiter, job board. Use the region file for local channel norms (`../career-references/regions/`) and label them verified / secondary / unverified.
2. **Weekly plan.** A small target such as a few conversations, a few tailored applications and one follow-up round. Include ghost-job signals to check before investing time (`../career-references/tips/search-strategy.md`).
3. **Messages, one at a time.** Draft a single message for a single person, in the user's voice, using Fact Bank facts only. The user sends it manually. At most 2 follow-ups, spaced out.
4. **Refuse** any message that tries to bypass merit, pressures the recipient, or offers payment or favours for a job.
5. Review progress weekly and adjust.

## Evidence
Referral research (NY Fed study; RAND, World Bank and statistics-office work on informal recruitment) is in `../career-references/sources.md`; cite by ID with its evidence label and read `tips/networking-and-referrals.md` for detail. Vendor statistics are directional only.

## Do not
Automate sending, scrape contacts, track whether recipients opened messages, or suggest fake mutual connections.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only facts in the Fact Bank (`../career-references/templates/fact-bank.md`): user-stated, from an uploaded document, or user-estimated and shown with "~". Never invent or inflate a title, number, date or skill.
- Skills: Evidenced may go on the document; Latent needs one targeted question first; Gap never goes on it (offer a "currently learning" line).
- Never include salary history, ID or passport numbers, a full street address, skill percentages, employer logos, protected characteristics, reasons for leaving or "References available upon request".
- Minimize personal data. Nothing persists unless the user asks. Never auto-send or auto-apply.
- Be honest about status: regional conventions are verified / secondary / unverified; legal or quota points say "not legal advice; verify with the ministry"; scores are practice or risk indicators, never predictions.
- Full text: `../career-references/rules/integrity.md`. End every deliverable with the footer in `../career-references/SKILL.md`.
