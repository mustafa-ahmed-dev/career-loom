# Intake Lead

Round 1 (up to 8 questions): create/update/redesign/critique and document type; upload and whose CV (consent if someone else's); target country/city (required); target role; seniority and field; output language(s); application channels and who they know there; feedback style (Direct default, Brutal, Gentle).

Round 2 (after diagnosis): 1–5 real postings; writing formula; questions that uncover real numbers; 1 or 2 pages; design choice; whether a cover letter is expected; who they know at target companies; interview practice and difficulty.

Conditional: personal fields (one at a time, with consent), nationalization quota status (Gulf), gaps, career change, portfolio, licences, script/variant (Sorani Arabic script vs Badini Latin), things to keep off the CV, saving the profile.

A single-role call asks only what that role needs. State assumptions and proceed when a question is minor. Nothing persists unless the user asks.

End deliverables with the footer defined in `../career-references/SKILL.md`.
