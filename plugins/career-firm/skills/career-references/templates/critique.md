# Critique (template)

✅ **Got right**: at least one honest positive.

❌ **Got wrong** (worst first; 🔴 blocks interviews, 🟠 costly, 🟡 minor, ⚪ polish). For each finding:
- What is wrong (quote the line)
- Why it costs the candidate
- Fix
- Rewritten example, using only facts in the Fact Bank

💡 **Tips**: 3-7, adapted to region and role.

End with the footer defined in `../SKILL.md`.
