# Writing Bullets

## Choose a formula (the user picks; XYZ is not mandatory)
| Formula | Structure | Best for | Example shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| XYZ [S017] | Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z | Measurable output: sales, engineering, operations | "Cut ticket backlog by ~30% in 2 months by introducing a triage queue" |
| CAR / PAR | Challenge or Problem → Action → Result | Problem-solving, turnarounds | "Facing repeated delivery delays, rebuilt the routing sheet; on-time rate rose to ~95%" |
| STAR (compressed) | Situation/Task → Action → Result | Bullets that link to interview stories | One line per story in your story bank |
| Scope-first | Scale + responsibility + outcome | Executives, public sector, NGOs | "Led a team of 12 across 3 branches serving 40k customers; …" |
| Qualitative evidence | Action + concrete artefact or recognition | Admin, healthcare, education, research | "Wrote the onboarding guide adopted across all departments" |
| Your own | Your template | Anyone | — |

## Questions to find your real numbers
- **Volume:** how many per day, week or month (tickets, orders, patients, students, lines of code reviewed)?
- **Frequency:** how often did you do it?
- **Time:** how long did it take before you, and after?
- **Money:** budget handled, cost saved, revenue influenced?
- **Quality:** error rate, complaint rate, rating?
- **Scale:** team size, number of sites, users, countries?
- **Baseline:** compared with what? Last year, the team average, the target?

If you can only estimate, write "~" in front of the number. If no number exists, use scope or recognition instead; never invent one. [S020, S039]

## The truth gate
- Every number, title and date must exist in your fact bank.
- Team results stay team results: write "contributed to" or "as part of a 5-person team".
- Expect every number to come up in an interview as "How did you measure that?"
- Exaggerated CVs tend to backfire in person: the reader pictures a level, then meets someone below it. (One job seeker's observation from Iraq/Kurdistan, S159, Weak.)

## Voice fidelity: phrases to replace
| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "Spearheaded" | "Led", "Started", "Set up" |
| "Leveraged synergies" | Say what was combined and what came out of it |
| "Results-driven professional" | Cut it, and show a result instead |
| "Responsible for" | Start with what you did |
| "Dynamic", "passionate", "go-getter" | Cut them, and show evidence instead |
| "Utilised" | "Used" |
| "Various", "multiple" | Give the number |
| "I am writing to express my keen interest" (cover letters) | Open with the role and why you fit |

Keep your own phrasing wherever it's clear. Generic writing is penalised more than AI use itself. [S026, S029, S033]

## Style rules
- Start each bullet with a strong past-tense verb (present tense for your current role).
- One idea per bullet, at most 2 lines.
- No "I" in US/UK résumé bullets. [S019]
- Put the most relevant 3–5 bullets first for each role.
- Tailor for every application; tailored résumés show higher interview rates in vendor data. [S034, Vendor]
