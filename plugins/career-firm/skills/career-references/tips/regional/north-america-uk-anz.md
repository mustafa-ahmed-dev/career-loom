# International English: US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand

## US and Canada
- Résumé of 1–2 pages, tailored to each job, achievement-heavy.
- No photo, date of birth or marital status (anti-discrimination law).
- Referrals were 11.6% of 2025 hires on Greenhouse. [S045]
- Interviews are structured and behavioural (STAR); big tech runs multi-interview loops. [S035, S040]
- Post-interview ghosting is common: 52% of US job seekers report it. [S044]
- Cover letters: US vendor surveys say most employers require or read them and most candidates skip them; a tailored letter beat both a generic letter and none in one field experiment. [S156–S158, Vendor]

## UK and Ireland
- CV of about 2 A4 pages.
- No photo or date of birth (equality practice, not a legal ban). [S089]
- Use British spelling and DD/MM/YYYY dates.
- Add a right-to-work line if relevant.
- Ghosting reported by 42% (UK) and 54% (Ireland). [S044]

## Australia and New Zealand (U)
- Résumé of 2–3 pages, often with referees listed.
- No photo.
- SEEK has no job-search API, so pasting the job description is the path. [S108]
