# UN and International NGOs

## UN (Inspira)
- You apply through an online Inspira profile, not a designed PDF. Fill the structured fields carefully, because automatic screening checks them against the job's criteria. [S041]
- The process: automatic screening → assessment exercise → competency-based interview. [S041]
- The interview is a panel, usually 30–60 minutes, asking for evidence of each competency from your past behaviour. [S041, S042]
- Prepare one STAR story per competency listed in the vacancy.
- Use the vacancy's exact competency names in your profile and answers.
- A motivation statement is expected; keep it specific to the mandate and the duty station.

## International NGOs
- Long-form CVs with duties plus results are common (use design P8 Public Service & Field).
- Name the donors and the scale of projects ("USD 2M EU-funded project, 3 governorates").
- Language skills matter: list working languages, using CEFR levels where possible.
- Expect competency-based or scenario interviews, sometimes with a written test.

## Iraq and the Kurdistan Region
NGOs and international NGOs are major employers there; see `iraq-kri.md`. ILO Job Search Clubs operate in Duhok and Ninawa. [S074]
