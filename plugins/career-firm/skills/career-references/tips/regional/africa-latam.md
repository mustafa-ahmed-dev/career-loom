# Sub-Saharan Africa and Latin America (light pack; unverified)

## Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa (U)
- CV of 2–3 pages; referees are often listed, with their consent.
- Photo uncommon in South Africa; optional elsewhere.
- Job boards: Jobberman (Nigeria), PNet (South Africa); personal networks matter.
- Data laws: POPIA (South Africa), NDPA (Nigeria).

## Latin America: Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia (U)
- Currículo (Brazil) or Hoja de vida (Spanish-speaking countries), 1–2 pages.
- Photo common, but optional.
- Job boards: Catho (Brazil), Computrabajo, InfoJobs Brazil.
- Hiring weighs relationships heavily (e.g., Brazil's "jeitinho" culture).
- Data law: LGPD (Brazil).
