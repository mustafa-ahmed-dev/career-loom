# Networking and Referrals

## Why this matters
- Referred candidates convert far better. In one large US firm, referrals were 6% of applicants but 29% of hires. [S057, Strong]
- People you know moderately well (about 10 mutual connections) produce the most job moves, more than close friends. [S059, Strong]
- In much of the Arab region, personal connections are often the main way people find jobs. [S063, S064, Strong]
- One job seeker in Iraq/Kurdistan reports that most of his jobs, and those of people he knows, came through referrals, with Telegram groups and channels, LinkedIn and Bayt as the next channels. [S159, Weak: n=1, unconfirmed]

## Build your warm-path list
1. Former colleagues and managers.
2. Classmates and university alumni.
3. People from training courses, conferences and professional associations.
4. Friends of friends who work in your field.
5. Recruiters who specialise in your field and city.
6. Telegram job groups and channels in your field (where used in your market): follow them, and message the person who posted the vacancy.

Mark each person by how well you know them, and focus on the middle: people who know you but aren't your closest friends.

## Message structures (written in your own words, in your chosen language)
- **Referral request:** how you know them → the specific role (link) → why you fit, in one line → a direct ask ("Would you be comfortable referring me or passing on my CV?") → make it easy to say no.
- **Informational conversation:** who you are → why them specifically → ask for 15 minutes → suggest two times.
- **Hiring manager / recruiter note:** the role → your single strongest proof → ask for a short conversation.
- **Thank-you:** within 24 hours, one specific thing you learned or appreciated.

## Channel notes
- Where employers contact candidates by WhatsApp or phone first (reported for Iraq/Kurdistan, S159, unconfirmed), keep your phone number in international format, make sure it's reachable on WhatsApp, and keep your CV PDF small enough to send easily.

## Follow-up schedule
- One polite follow-up after 7–10 working days.
- One final note about a week later.
- Then stop. Two follow-ups at most.

## The referral packet (make it easy for the person referring you)
- A forwardable paragraph about you (3–4 lines).
- A tailored CV.
- A link to the job.

## Ethics
- Asking someone to vouch for you or refer you is legitimate.
- The plugin refuses to draft messages that ask anyone to skip the hiring process or that offer payment or favours. [S065]
- One message at a time, sent by you. No mass-messaging.

## Round 3 additions (October 2026)
- Iraq/KRI evidence: employers recruit mostly through informal networks (RAND 2014); job seekers under-use formal channels because they believe jobs come through connections (World Bank); most firms report informal recruitment (KRSO 2022). [S196–S198]
- Wasta is linked to employment status and income across five Arab countries. [S199]
- Do not write messages that ask to bypass merit or offer payment.
