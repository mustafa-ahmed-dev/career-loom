# Design and Layout

## Pick a design by where the file is going
| Tier | Looks like | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Plain, single column | Any portal or ATS upload |
| **B** | Single column with colour | Most portals; direct sends |
| **C** | Sidebar or two columns | Sending directly to people (email, referrals, career fairs) |
| **D** | Graphic or portfolio | Portfolio and direct sends only (PDF) |

**Rule:** anything uploaded to a portal must be tier A. Tier C and D designs always come with an A-tier twin. [S011, S008]

**Local employers that read CVs by hand:** where HR reads CVs manually rather than through ATS software (reported for Iraq/Kurdistan, S159, unconfirmed), tier B and C designs are acceptable for direct sends. Keep the A twin for multinationals, NGOs, the UN and any online portal.

## Readability basics
- Body text at least 10 pt (11 pt for Arabic-script languages).
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).
- Never use colour alone to carry meaning.
- One accent colour is usually enough.
- Generous margins; don't cram content to fit one page.

## Visual elements
- **Icons:** always next to a text label.
- **Skill levels:** written in text (Expert / Advanced / Working / Basic). No percentages.
- **Charts:** design-portfolio styles only, built from real data, with a text version alongside.
- **QR code:** always print the URL next to it.
- **No employer logos.**

## Three design suggestions
The plugin suggests three options:
1. **Safe pick:** tier A, best for your region.
2. **Best fit:** matched to your region, industry and seniority.
3. **Stretch:** regional, bilingual or creative. If your favourite fails a check, it goes here with a warning.
