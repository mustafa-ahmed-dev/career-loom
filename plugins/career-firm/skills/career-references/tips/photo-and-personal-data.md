# Photo and Personal Data

## Photo rule
- **The photo is optional everywhere.** The photo slot is hidden by default.
- **The plugin suggests a photo only when:**
  - the role depends on appearance: actors, models, TV presenters, cabin crew, brand ambassadors, fitness trainers, on-camera creators, some front-of-house hospitality roles; or
  - the job posting asks for one.
- **Many people add a photo anyway, which is usually unnecessary.** (One job seeker's observation from Iraq/Kurdistan, S159, Weak.) The plugin says "common here, still optional" and leaves the choice to the user.
- **Actors and models:** use a separate professional headshot rather than a photo on the résumé (industry norm; round 3 will verify).
- **Cabin crew:** airlines usually ask for photos to their own specification in their application portal (verify per airline).
- **Regional conventions** are given as information only, never pressure:
  - Photos are common in Germany/Austria/Switzerland, France, Spain, the Gulf, Iraq, China, Japan, Korea and Latin America.
  - Photos are discouraged in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Ireland. [S089, S090, S080]
- **Fixed forms:**
  - **Japan rirekisho:** the photo slot is expected, and leaving it empty may be read negatively. [S093, Weak]
  - **Korea:** fair-hiring and blind-hiring rules restrict photo requests (details to be verified in round 3).
- The ATS-safe twin leaves the photo out by default.

## If you use a photo
- Recent; head and shoulders.
- Plain background, good light.
- Professional clothing.
- No selfies, filters or cropped group photos.
- Compressed, so the PDF stays small.

## Personal data
- **Never include:** government ID, passport or Iqama numbers; your full street address (city and country are enough); salary history.
- **Include only when your target region expects it, and only if you agree to each one:** nationality, visa status, date of birth, marital status. [S080]
- **Iraq/Kurdistan default (founder observation, S159):** nationality on; marital status off unless the employer requires it.
- The plugin never adds religion, health or similar characteristics.

## Round 3 additions (October 2026)
- Hidden by default is confirmed: field experiments in Israel, Germany, Spain and Italy show photos can help or hurt depending on gender, name, headscarf and weight, so the plugin never inserts one on its own. [S160–S163]
- Korea: employers with 30 or more regular staff may not ask for appearance, height, weight, birthplace, marital status, property or family details; an ID photo is still allowed. Not legal advice; verify. [S165–S167]
- Japan: the 2021 MHLW model rirekisho made gender optional and removed commuting time, dependants and spouse fields. Photo dimensions on the current form are unconfirmed. [S168–S171]
- US: EEOC guidance says employers should not ask for a photo before an offer. [S164]
- Region-scoped defaults: date of birth, nationality and marital status are hidden by default and shown on request in GCC, Iraq, Turkey and Japan packs where they are customary.
