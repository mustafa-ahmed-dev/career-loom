# Asia (light pack; mostly unverified)

## Japan
- Two documents:
  - **Rirekisho:** a standard form. Photo slot expected (3×4 cm, taken within 3 months). [S091–S093]
  - **Shokumu keirekisho:** a free-format career history, often 2–4 pages. [S092]
- The old JIS sample form was reportedly replaced in 2020 by an MHLW standard form. Verify. [S094]
- Hiring runs through agencies and Hello Work; expect a formal process with several rounds.
- Global firms accept an English CV.

## China (U)
- 个人简历, 1–2 pages; photo and date of birth common, but optional.
- Job boards: BOSS Zhipin, Zhaopin. Personal connections (guanxi) matter.
- Data law: PIPL.

## South Korea (U)
- 이력서 (résumé) + 자기소개서 (self-introduction essay).
- Blind-hiring and fair-hiring rules restrict photo and personal-data requests. Round 3 will verify the details.
- Job boards: Saramin (official API after approval) [S113], JobKorea.
- Data law: PIPA.

## India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka (U)
- 2-page résumé. The plugin strips "biodata" personal fields (date of birth, religion, marital status) by default; the user can add them back.
- A declaration line is common on CVs in some sectors.
- Job boards: Naukri (no public API; do not scrape) [S118], BDJobs; referrals.
- Hiring often starts with aptitude tests, then HR and technical interviews.
- Data law: India's DPDP Act 2023.

## Southeast Asia: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines, Vietnam (U)
- About 2 pages. Photo common in the Philippines and Indonesia, but optional.
- Job boards: JobStreet and Jobsdb (now on the SEEK platform, with no job-search API). [S112, S108]
- Data laws: PDPA (Singapore and Malaysia).

## Turkey and Iran (U)
- **Turkey:** Özgeçmiş (CV), photo common; Kariyer.net; data law KVKK.
- **Iran:** Persian CV, photo common. Sanctions may limit access to some tools.
