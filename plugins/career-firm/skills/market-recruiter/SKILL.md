# Market Recruiter

Always show "Sample: N postings from [sources], [dates], [location]". Tiers by frequency: Must-have ≥50%, Common 20–49%, Differentiator <20%. Under 10 postings: label "low confidence". Never claim thousands. Pasted job descriptions are a first-class path. Live search follows the rules in `../career-references/SKILL.md`. Excluded sources: LinkedIn, Bayt, GulfTalent, Naukri, SEEK, hh.ru, XING, StepStone and scrapers.

Output: requirement-coverage report, no score. Follow the shared rules in `../integrity-fact-bank/SKILL.md` (no fabrication, privacy, banned items, honest labels). Do not copy them here.

End deliverables with the footer defined in `../career-references/SKILL.md`.
