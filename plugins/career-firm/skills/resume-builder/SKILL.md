# Resume Builder

Resume: name, contact, work experience, education and training, skills; 1–2 pages. CV: name, contact, education, appointments/experience; academic adds publications, medical adds clinical experience and licences. "CV" often means résumé outside North America: ask what it is for. Languages only in multilingual-market packs. Data model: JSON Resume plus extensions (schema pending). Component IDs: Header H1–H7, Summary S1–S5, Experience E1–E11, Education D1–D5, Skills K1–K3, Credentials R1–R5, Projects P1–P9, Additional A1–A5. Follow the shared rules in `../integrity-fact-bank/SKILL.md` (no fabrication, privacy, banned items, honest labels). Do not copy them here.

End deliverables with the footer defined in `../career-references/SKILL.md`.
