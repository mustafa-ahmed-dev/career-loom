# Career Loom: privacy

- The plugin minimises personal data and asks consent before using someone else's CV.
- Nothing is stored by default. A "forget me" option is offered.
- It never sends messages, applies to jobs or shares links on your behalf.
- Documents are written to a cloud destination (Google Drive, Microsoft 365, Notion) only when you ask.
- Web search (when you turn it on) sends search terms, not your CV, to the search tool. Live findings are never saved into Career Loom's library.
- Interview scores are practice feedback only. Legal and quota statements are not legal advice.
