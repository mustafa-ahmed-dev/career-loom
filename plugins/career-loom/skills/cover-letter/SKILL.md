# Cover Letter

## Ask first
Whether a letter is expected for this employer and country. Evidence is thin: the one dataset in the library is US-only vendor data (ResumeGo: tailored 16.4%, generic 12.5%, none 10.7%) and there is no regional data, so never present it as a rule. Then ask for the posting, why this company, and one or two proof points. Check the region file in `../career-references/regions/` for the local norm; in some markets a short WhatsApp or email intro replaces a formal letter.

## Parts
Sender header and date, recipient, salutation, opening (the role and one specific reason), proof paragraph (one or two Fact Bank achievements tied to the posting), closing with a call to action, sign-off. No charts. Keep it to one page; an intro message is 3-5 lines.

## Steps
1. Pull facts from the Fact Bank only. If there is none, run the Fact Bank step from `cv-write` first.
2. Draft in the user's voice. No stock openings, no flattery, no claims the CV does not support.
3. Non-English letters go through `localization`.
4. Show the draft and a short list of what you chose and why. The user sends it themselves; never send on their behalf.

## Read when needed
`../career-references/tips/cover-letters.md`.

## Non-negotiables
- Use only facts in the Fact Bank (`../career-references/templates/fact-bank.md`): user-stated, from an uploaded document, or user-estimated and shown with "~". Never invent or inflate a title, number, date or skill.
- Skills: Evidenced may go on the document; Latent needs one targeted question first; Gap never goes on it (offer a "currently learning" line).
- Never include salary history, ID or passport numbers, a full street address, skill percentages, employer logos, protected characteristics, reasons for leaving or "References available upon request".
- Minimize personal data. Nothing persists unless the user asks. Never auto-send or auto-apply.
- Be honest about status: regional conventions are verified / secondary / unverified; legal or quota points say "not legal advice; verify with the ministry"; scores are practice or risk indicators, never predictions.
- Full text: `../career-references/rules/integrity.md`. End every deliverable with the footer in `../career-references/SKILL.md`.
