# P2 Modern Minimal

ATS tier: **A** (plain single column). Status: finished. Tokens: `tokens.json` (schema in `systems/TOKENS.md`).

## Use it for
Portals and people alike, when you want a calm, spacious page. Good for design, product and creative-adjacent roles where plain does not mean dense.

## Layout
One column, A4, generous margins (18-20 mm) and line height 1.42. Headings are plain semibold text, not upper case and not ruled. Bullets are dashes. Black and grey only.

## Fonts (OFL, by role)
Inter (Latin, Cyrillic) with IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic script; Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans Devanagari (downloaded on demand) for Chinese and Devanagari. Mono role: IBM Plex Mono.

## RTL variant
`--rtl auto` follows the CV language. Plain, spacious headings; the page mirrors fully. Emails, URLs and phone numbers stay left-to-right inside the line. Arabic uses a taller line height and no ligatures so that extracted text keeps its letter order. Sorani is labelled **needs native review**.

## Rules this design never breaks
No skill bars or percentages, no logos, no photo, contact details in the body text (never only in a header or footer), no information only in a graphic.
