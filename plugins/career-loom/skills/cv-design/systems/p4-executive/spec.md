# P4 Executive

ATS tier: **A** (plain single column). Status: finished. Tokens: `tokens.json` (schema in `systems/TOKENS.md`).

## Use it for
Senior and management CVs: strong name, restrained structure, summary first.

## Layout
One column. Serif name, sans-serif section headings in upper case with a rule above each section, wide margins (22 mm sides). Black and grey only.

## Fonts (OFL, by role)
Name in Source Serif 4 (Latin) / Noto Naskh Arabic; headings and body in Inter / IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans Devanagari downloaded on demand. Mono role: IBM Plex Mono.

## RTL variant
`--rtl auto` follows the CV language. Rules above sections mirror naturally. Emails, URLs and phone numbers stay left-to-right inside the line. Arabic uses a taller line height and no ligatures so that extracted text keeps its letter order. Sorani is labelled **needs native review**.

## Rules this design never breaks
No skill bars or percentages, no logos, no photo, contact details in the body text (never only in a header or footer), no information only in a graphic.

## Word output
Yes: single column, same section order, real headings and bullets (verified in LibreOffice only, not Word).
