# C20 Portfolio Lite

ATS tier: **D** (graphic or portfolio, PDF only). Status: finished. Tokens: `tokens.json` (schema in `systems/TOKENS.md`). A-rated plain twin: **P1 Plain Master**, produced automatically.

## Use it for
Creative and technical roles with projects to show. PDF only, sent to a person; **never to a portal**: the P1 twin is produced automatically.

## Layout
An indigo header band, then projects shown as two-column cards (pale blue-grey, with a thin border) ahead of work history. Every card is text only: title, organisation, dates and bullets. Project links print as visible text, so they survive print.

## Fonts (OFL, by role)
Inter (Latin, Cyrillic) with IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic script; Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans Devanagari (downloaded on demand) for Chinese and Devanagari. Mono role: IBM Plex Mono.

## RTL variant
`--rtl auto` follows the CV language. Cards fill from the right in RTL. Emails, URLs and phone numbers stay left-to-right inside the line. Arabic uses a taller line height and no ligatures so that extracted text keeps its letter order. Sorani is labelled **needs native review**.

## Rules this design never breaks
No skill bars or percentages, no logos, no photo, contact details in the body text (never only in a header or footer), no information only in a graphic.
