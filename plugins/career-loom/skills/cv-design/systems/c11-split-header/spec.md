# C11 Split Header

ATS tier: **C** (two columns or grid, for people). Status: finished. Tokens: `tokens.json` (schema in `systems/TOKENS.md`). A-rated plain twin: **P1 Plain Master**, produced automatically.

## Use it for
CVs sent to a person where a clear header and a light skills column suit the content. **Never upload it to a portal**: the P1 twin is produced automatically.

## Layout
A split header: name and headline at the start, contact details stacked at the end, burgundy rule below. Under it a 50 mm column holds skills, languages and certificates beside the main column, separated by a thin rule. No tint.

## Fonts (OFL, by role)
Inter (Latin, Cyrillic) with IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic script; Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans Devanagari (downloaded on demand) for Chinese and Devanagari. Mono role: IBM Plex Mono.

## RTL variant
`--rtl auto` follows the CV language. Name and contact swap sides; the skills column moves to the right. Emails, URLs and phone numbers stay left-to-right inside the line. Arabic uses a taller line height and no ligatures so that extracted text keeps its letter order. Sorani is labelled **needs native review**.

## Rules this design never breaks
No skill bars or percentages, no logos, no photo, contact details in the body text (never only in a header or footer), no information only in a graphic.
