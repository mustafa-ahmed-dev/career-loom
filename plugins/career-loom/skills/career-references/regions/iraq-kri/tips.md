# Iraq and Kurdistan Region (only what published sources support)

## Labour market
- 37.9% of employment is in the public sector (70.5% for women). [S070]
- Youth unemployment is 35.8%. [S070]
- In the Kurdistan Region, 71.3% of employed young people work in private companies. [S073]
- **There is no representative data on how people in Iraq or Kurdistan actually find jobs.** Tell users this honestly.

## Where jobs are found
- Personal networks.
- NGO and international NGO portals.
- LinkedIn posts, Facebook and Telegram groups, WhatsApp, and email requests for CVs. [S077]
- Local job boards: HireKurd [S078], Bayt (a thin market: about 30 Kurdistan postings in one 2025 snapshot) [S076].
- The KRG job portal (since November 2024; private sector only at launch). [S075]
- ILO Job Search Clubs in Duhok and Ninawa. [S074]

## The CV
- Photo: optional (see `../../tips/photo-and-personal-data.md`).
- Marital status sometimes included (weak source). [S079]
- Never include national ID numbers.
