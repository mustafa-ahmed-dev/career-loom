# Levant, Egypt and Maghreb (light pack)

## Levant (Jordan, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria)

**Hiring channels**
- Personal connections dominate: over 70% of workers in Lebanon and Syria found jobs mainly this way. [S063]
- 53% of Palestinians say wasta is frequently needed to get a job. [S064]
- In Jordan, 65% consider wasta necessary to get a job, even though 83% call it corruption. [S066]

**The CV**
- 1–2 pages, in English, French (Lebanon) or Arabic.
- Photo common, but optional.

## Egypt (U)
- CV in English, 1–2 pages.
- Photo common, but optional.
- Male applicants may include a military-status line.
- Main channels: Wuzzuf, Forasna and referrals.

## Maghreb (U)
- French-style CV, about 1 page, in a formal French register.
- Photo common, but optional.
- Main channels: Rekrute (Morocco) and referrals.
