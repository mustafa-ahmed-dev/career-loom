# Gulf (GCC): Saudi Arabia and UAE first

## The CV
- About 2 pages.
- Photo common, but optional.
- Nationality and visa status near the top. [S080]
- Never include passport or Iqama numbers.

## Hiring
- Referrals are important. [S068]
- Many jobs come through recruitment agencies.
- Common hurdles: visa and work permits. [S069]

## Nationalisation quotas (not legal advice; verify with the ministry)

**Saudi Arabia: Nitaqat**
- New phase 2026–2028 targeting more than 340,000 jobs.
- 100% Saudi nationals required in 69 administrative roles from 5 April 2026.
- Quotas for other professions also apply.
- A Saudi employee must earn at least SAR 4,000 to count towards the quota. [S084]

**UAE: Emiratisation**
- Firms with 50+ staff must reach 10% Emiratis in skilled roles by 31 December 2026. [S085]
- Smaller firms in 14 sectors have their own rules. [S086]

**Before tailoring a CV for Saudi Arabia,** check whether the role is reserved for nationals. Every GCC market brief must include a dated localisation check.
