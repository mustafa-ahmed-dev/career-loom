# Job-Search Strategy

## The honest picture
- Most online applications get no reply; in one large data set only about 3 in 100 applicants were invited to interview. [S052, Moderate]
- Some postings aren't active hiring: about 18–22% on one major platform. [S045, Moderate]
- Silence is usually about volume and timing, not about your worth.

## A weekly plan (example; adjust to your situation)
| Activity | Target per week |
|---|---|
| Tailored applications | 5 |
| Outreach or referral messages | 5 |
| Informational conversations | 1 |
| Follow-ups | All that are due |
| Skill-gap work | 2–3 hours |

**Measure progress by conversations and interviews per week, not by the number of applications sent.**

## Signs a posting may not be active (signals, not proof)
- Reposted again and again for months.
- No named team, manager or specific project.
- Vague "always hiring" or "talent pool" wording.
- The same role is open in many cities at once.

When you see these, spend less time on that posting and look for a person to contact there instead.

## Pair every application with a person
For each application, ask: "Who could I contact at this company or near it?" Even one message raises your odds.
