# Banned claims

Check every statistic or headline claim against this list before stating it. If it matches, do not use it, even when a live search result repeats it. Use the correction instead.

| # | Banned claim | Use instead | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Cover letters: 125,000 applications" (misquoted study) | ResumeGo field experiment, US-only vendor data: tailored 16.4% / generic 12.5% / none 10.7%; no MENA data (S156; myths-vs-reality #24) | Principle 9 |
| 2 | "Compared against thousands of job descriptions" | "Sample: N postings from [sources], [dates], [location]"; under 10 postings = low confidence | Principle 6 |
| 3 | Any "ATS score" or "ATS pass rate" | Parse-Risk & Findability Index, presented as risks | Principle 6 |
| 4 | "XYZ is the formula Google/Meta use" | XYZ (Bock, 2014) as one option among CAR, PAR, STAR, scope-first, qualitative and the user's own | Principle 7 |
| 5 | "Most job postings are fake" | Some postings are ghost jobs: roughly 18–22% on Greenhouse data (vendor, directional) | Principle 4 |
| 6 | "75% of résumés are rejected by ATS before a human sees them" | Not supported: see myths-vs-reality #1 (S001–S003); the figure traces to a 2012 sales pitch | Confirmed in myths-vs-reality #1 |
| 7 | Output is "undetectable" | Never claimed | Principle 8 |
| 8 | "Our ATS score is X%" or "75% match rate guarantees interviews" (vendor targets) | Requirement-coverage report, no score. Jobscan's 75% target is an unverified vendor construct (S225) | Principle 6 |
| 9 | "Recruiters spend 6 seconds on a résumé" stated as fact | Vendor eye-tracking study: about 7.4 seconds in the 2018 update, sample size undisclosed (S181); other studies report longer times (S183). Directional only | Principle on vendor stats |
| 10 | "Cover letters raise interview chances by X%" for Iraq/KRI or the Arab region | No MENA data exists. US-only vendor data only (S156–S158) | Principle 9 |
| 11 | "Referrals are most hiring in Iraq/KRI" as a measured figure | Labour-market sources report informal recruitment is common (S196–S198); no representative channel-share figure exists | Principle 4 |
| 12 | "Naukrigulf/GulfTalent/Bayt premium boosts you X times" | Unverified vendor claims (S220–S222); the plugin builds no recruiter-visibility boosts | Rejected features |
| 13 | "Attractive photos always help" or "photos always hurt" | Evidence is mixed and country-specific (S160–S163); photo is optional and hidden by default | Photo rule |

When a new bad figure keeps appearing in search results, add a row here during the quarterly review.
